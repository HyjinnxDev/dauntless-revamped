## Docs site (`versions/1.4.4/docs/`)

Jekyll site (`remote_theme: just-the-docs` v0.12.0, `baseurl: /dauntless-revived`, published at `mixutin.github.io`). English pages at the root; Finnish translations under `fi/` share the same path and are paired by front-matter `ref`.

| `_includes/faq_resolve.html` | Resolves page links inside FAQ answers from `_data/faq_<lang>.yml` |
